# Legal Documentation

## Purpose

`docs/legal/` stores the service-level agreements, edition tier matrix, and
other legal terms that govern commercial use of SDKWork Claw Router. These
documents define availability commitments, incident response times, service
credits, rate-limit tiers, support channels, and the comparison matrix across
commercial editions.

## Owner

SDKWork commercial and legal team. Contact `sales@sdkwork.com` for
commercial authorization and `legal@sdkwork.com` for legal inquiries.

## Allowed Content

Service Level Agreements, edition tier matrices, legal terms that reference
the commercial license and pricing model, and references to compliance
evidence.

## Forbidden Content

Generated SDK transport output, live credentials, private customer contract
terms, individual customer data, and copied root standards.
