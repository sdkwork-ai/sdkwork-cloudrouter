# Commercial Documentation

## Purpose

`docs/commercial/` stores the commercial pricing and licensing model for
SDKWork Claw Router. These documents describe the public commercial tiers,
token metering, additional services pricing, payment methods, and refund
policy that apply to commercial editions.

## Owner

SDKWork commercial team. Contact `sales@sdkwork.com` for commercial
authorization and `billing@sdkwork.com` for billing inquiries.

## Allowed Content

Commercial pricing tables, license tier definitions, token metering rules,
additional service rate cards, payment and refund policies, and references to
the legal SLA and tier matrix.

## Forbidden Content

Internal cost structures, supplier contract terms, individual customer
contract terms, negotiated discounts not approved for public publication,
and any private customer data.
